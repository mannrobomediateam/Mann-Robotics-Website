Meet Our ADC and ADC PRO Teams

What is ADC & ADC PRO?

The Aerial Drone Competition (ADC) by the REC Foundation challenges students to design, build, and pilot drones through thrilling engineering challenges. From newcomers to elite competitors, ADC and ADC Pro provide hands-on STEM experience, teamwork, and innovation — pushing students to reach new heights in drone technology and problem-solving.

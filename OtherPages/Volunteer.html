Looking to Volunteer?

Available Roles:
  • Queue Table:

    As a Queue Table volunteer, you will be responsible for ensuring that all teams are checked in for their match at the proper field before the match start

  • Scorekeeper:

    As a scorekeeper, you will be responsible for keeping track of tallying up scores after each match and communicating those scores to the teams and the referees. You may also be responsible for resetting field elements between matches. Phone or handheld device required
